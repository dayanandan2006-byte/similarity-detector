Automated Integrity
VIEW: v_flagged_duplicates ≥ 80% Filter

Filters proposals where highest_similarity_score ≥ 80.0 or status = 'Flagged Duplicate', joined with matched proposal and lead author info.

VIEW: v_domain_analytics Aggregation

Aggregates domain-wise proposal totals, duplicate risk rates, and average similarity scores across all research tracks.

TRIGGER: trg_proposal_status_audit AFTER UPDATE

Automatically inserts an immutable audit log whenever a proposal's approval or review status changes.

TRIGGER: trg_similarity_threshold_check AFTER INSERT

When an evaluation log records score ≥ 80.0%, automatically updates proposal status to 'Flagged Duplicate' and logs high-risk alert.
